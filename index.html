What we do

Corpers serving beyond the uniform.

We combine education with practical action, helping young people understand hygiene while improving the environments where they learn, play and live.

🧼

Personal Hygiene Education

We teach boys and girls practical habits around personal cleanliness, healthy living and everyday hygiene.
